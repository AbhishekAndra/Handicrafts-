CraftMarket connects independent artisans with people who care where their things come from — and who made them.

Our Story

CraftMarket started with a simple frustration: extraordinary craftspeople were selling remarkable work at local markets for a fraction of what it was worth, while shoppers online were wading through mass-produced items pretending to be handmade.

So we built a marketplace with one rule — every listing is made by the person selling it. Artisans set their own prices, tell their own stories, and keep the overwhelming majority of every sale.

Today, potters, weavers, jewellers and woodcarvers across the world use CraftMarket to reach customers who value the hours their hands put in.

What We Stand For

Makers First

Artisans set their prices and keep 92% of every sale. No hidden fees, no forced discounts.

Genuinely Handmade

Every seller is verified. If it was not made by the person listing it, it does not belong here.

Made to Last

Natural materials, plastic-free packaging, and carbon-offset shipping on every order.

Traditions Preserved

We fund apprenticeships so techniques passed down for generations keep finding new hands.
